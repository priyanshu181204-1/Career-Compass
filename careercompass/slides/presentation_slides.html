🧭 CareerCompass

Resume Intelligence & Job Matching Platform

An end-to-end recruitment intelligence system combining a machine learning classifier trained on 10,600+ structured job postings with an NLP and RAG retrieval layer over unstructured candidate resumes and technical knowledge bases.

Domain: Recruitment Technology & HR Analytics  |  Cohort: CSE VII, Batch 1  |  Date: September 2026
Problem Statement & Motivation

The Automated Screening Dilemma

Bridging the opaque gap between Applicant Tracking Systems and candidate career growth.

⚠️ The Current Industry Reality

  • Over 75% of resumes are rejected by automated ATS systems within seconds.
  • Rejected candidates receive generic rejection templates with zero actionable feedback.
  • Keyword mismatch (e.g. "K8s" vs "Kubernetes") unfairly drops qualified applicants.
  • Recruiters lack transparent, explainable feature attribution for why candidates match.

💡 The CareerCompass Solution

  • Transparent 0-100 Match Score with 4-part visible calculation breakdown.
  • Ranked Missing Skills quantified by marginal score impact points.
  • Grounded 4-Week Remediation Roadmap citing specific docs, books, and courses.
  • Autonomous Conversational Agent with database query tool calling.

🛡️ Metric Defense: Why Balanced F1 Matters Most

Recruiters prioritize Precision to prevent interviewing false positives; candidates require Recall so qualified profiles are not rejected. For CareerCompass, Balanced F1-Score is paramount because it simultaneously penalizes misleading match endorsements and unfair applicant rejections.

Text Processing & NLP

Dense Semantic Embeddings vs. TF-IDF

Resolving the vocabulary mismatch problem between resumes and job descriptions.

TF-IDF Baseline (Lexical N-Grams)

  • Matches exact token and character sequences.
  • Fails completely on abbreviations and synonyms:
    "K8s, PSQL, Golang, AWS" vs "Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Go"
  • Cosine Similarity Score: 0.0000 (False Negative)

Dense Semantic Embeddings (LSA / SVD)

  • Projects text into continuous latent semantic space.
  • Captures conceptual equivalence and industry synonyms.
  • Cosine Similarity Score: 0.3816 (+0.3816 Gain)
  • Correctly recognizes semantic alignment despite zero word overlap.
AI Layer & RAG

Knowledge Base Retrieval & Tool Calling

Autonomous assistant grounded in relational SQL data and engineering curricula.

RAG Improvement Roadmaps

For detected skill gaps, retrieves curated learning resources and synthesizes an actionable 4-week plan:

  • Week 1: Fundamentals & official docs citations.
  • Week 2: Certified coursework (CKA, Coursera, Udemy).
  • Week 3: Hands-on production capstone projects.
  • Week 4: Mock interview Q&A with evaluation points.

Implemented Tool Calling Functions

  • tool_query_jobs(): Filters 10,600+ jobs by role, location, salary.
  • tool_get_skill_stats(): Computes real-time demand % and top hiring companies.
  • tool_get_interview_prep(): Retrieves technical interview Q&A.
  • tool_execute_sql(): Safe read-only analytical query execution.

Conclusion & Future Work

Limitations & Strategic Roadmap

System Limitations

  • Static Taxonomies: Newly emerging frameworks require periodic taxonomy graph updates.
  • Experience Estimation: Career gap detection relies on explicit date ranges.
  • Synthetic Data Ceiling: Synthetic resumes lack subtle informal soft-skill nuances.

Future Extensions

  • Fine-Tuned LLM Embeddings: Integration with domain-specialized recruiting embeddings.
  • Automated Interviewer Bot: Interactive audio-based technical screening mock interviews.
  • Recruiter Multi-Tenant Portal: Role-based access control for HR enterprise teams.
